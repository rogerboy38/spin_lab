⚰️ Gambler's Ruin — how it actually ends

cashed out went broke goal & bankroll lines

Classic theorem: in a fair game your chance of reaching the goal before ruin is exactly bankroll ÷ goal. Any house edge bends every path downward — and the longer anyone plays, the closer ruin gets to certain. This is the math no strategy escapes.

Jurisdiction reality: US states set a regulated, certified minimum RTP every machine must meet — Nevada 75%, New Jersey 83% (actual averages ~92–95%). Mexico's framework sets no mandated payout floor, so effective RTP can sit at 85% or lower. Compare ruin at "Nevada min · 75%" vs "loose market · 85%" to see how a thinner payout speeds the end.
